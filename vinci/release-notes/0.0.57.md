This release makes first use clearer and keeps Crew's saved work and completion states honest.

**Vero leaves room for your work.** The compact welcome shows Vinci's version, your project, connection state and next action. Small terminals keep a readable composer. The new image view remains an optional preview; plain and accessible views keep text status.

**Sign-in explains what happens next.** Vinci sign-in, provider accounts and API keys have clear choices and cancellation or failure messages. A saved credential is reported as saved; a successful model connection is reported after a successful response.

**Crew preserves unfinished work.** Cancelled and failed helper turns keep their partial changes for review and stay unfinished, including when the final history read fails. Cancelled partial changes need fresh approval. Applying saved work checks the files and proposal again after your decision, so a change made while the dialog is open cannot silently overwrite newer work.

**Recovery points to the right action.** Authentication, access, balance, capacity and sandbox failures retain their reason and show the matching next step. Starting another turn does not erase an unresolved blocker.

**Piped output stays usable.** Print, JSON and RPC output, saved transcripts and HTML exports keep the conversation without terminal artwork or welcome decoration.

**Fortissimo uses Forte.** Vinci Fortissimo now uses Vinci Forte at Forte pricing. It is no longer offered as a separate model, and the Advisor no longer escalates from Forte to a stronger model. Existing Fortissimo settings and sessions keep working.
